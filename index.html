Short answers.Explore FAQs +
Is this a separate phone number?+

Yes. Your plumbing business receives a dedicated AI receptionist number. The AI answers calls sent to that number and gathers the details your team needs for follow-up.

Can I keep my current business number?+

Yes. Keep your current number and choose whether to forward selected calls to the dedicated AI number. We review the forwarding options and configuration with you during setup.

Does my office team still take calls?+

That is up to your routing setup. Your office can continue answering the current business line, with selected overflow or after-hours calls forwarded to the AI number. You can also use the dedicated number directly.

How are urgent plumbing calls handled?+

The receptionist captures the issue, location, contact information, and urgency using your intake rules. Your team remains responsible for callbacks, dispatch, and the service response. It is not an emergency dispatch replacement.

Can I choose the jobs and areas we cover?+

Yes. We configure your service areas, accepted plumbing jobs, intake questions, and handoff preferences. Callers are qualified against the rules you provide.

What does the free audit include?+

We review how your business currently receives calls, where missed or after-hours calls may be slipping through, and where a dedicated AI receptionist number could fit. We explain setup if the service looks suitable.
